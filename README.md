# 📝 PYQ OMR Practice App

A simple, browser-based OMR practice tool designed for students solving Previous Year Questions (PYQs) from a separate PDF.

Record your answers, enter the answer key after completing the test, and instantly evaluate your performance.

## 🚀 Live Demo

**Try the app here:**  
👉 https://nitin-chem.github.io/pyq-omr-practice/

## ✨ Features

- **MCQ Practice:** Record answers using options A, B, C, and D.
- **Numerical Answer Questions (NAT):** Switch individual questions to numerical-answer mode.
- **Question Navigation:** Move between questions and track your progress.
- **Answer Key Evaluation:** Enter the correct answers after finishing your practice session.
- **Performance Analysis:** Review correct, incorrect, and unanswered questions.
- **Accuracy Calculation:** Check your overall performance.
- **Question-wise Review:** Compare your answers with the answer key.
- **CSV Export:** Download your results for future analysis.
- **Compact Mode:** Use a smaller interface while working alongside your question paper.
